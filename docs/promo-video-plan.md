# NwSSU Faculty Evaluation System — Promotional Video Plan

Oct 7, 2026 · @Anghel

## 1. Concept

**Goal:** show, in about 90 seconds, how the system replaces the paper evaluation: students and supervisors rate faculty on their phones, and the evaluation office gets live results and ready-to-print CMO No. 19 reports.

**Audience:** the capstone panel, the NwSSU evaluation office, deans and program chairs, and students.

**Length:** a main video of 90 seconds, plus a 30-second cut for Facebook and TikTok.

**Tone:** modern, clean and confident, with a short, relatable "before" and a bright "after". Calm music that builds; no drama.

### Key messages

Keep every scene tied to one of these five:

1. **Easy:** students evaluate on their phone in a few minutes.
2. **Fast:** results reach the office live, with no paper tallying.
3. **Compliant:** follows CHED CMO No. 19, s. 2025: SET, SEF, Annex C, Annex D and the FER.
4. **Secure and anonymous:** personal sign-in, and faculty never see who rated them.
5. **Reliable:** works on weak signal, sends reminders, and several staff can work at once.

### Tagline options

- **"Rate. Reflect. Rise."** (suggested; echoes the university's RISE values)
- "Faculty evaluation, from paper to phone."
- "Every voice counted. Every result on time."
- "Evaluate anytime. Report in real time."

## 4. Shot list

### Prepare the demo data first

Use made-up records only, so no real names, IDs or ratings appear on camera.

- [ ] An active school year and semester, with the evaluation period open and a deadline set.
- [ ] A demo department with 3–4 faculty with realistic made-up names, and one program chair.
- [ ] 3–4 subjects, each with a teacher and enrolled students.
- [ ] Demo student A (password still = ID) to show sign-in and the password change.
- [ ] Demo student B with 2–3 pending teachers, to show evaluating.
- [ ] Several evaluations already submitted, so the dashboard, Annex C and FER have numbers.
- [ ] Signatories filled in with demo names, so the PDFs show Prepared by and Reviewed by.
- [ ] Phone: notifications allowed, Do Not Disturb on, battery above 50%, a clean status bar.

### Android app

1. Opening the app: the green splash screen with the seal.
2. Sign-in: typing the Student ID and password.
3. Choose a password: typing, then tapping the eye icon to show it.
4. Student dashboard: subjects, teachers, and Evaluated / Remaining counts.
5. Evaluating one teacher: scroll the statements, tap ratings, type a comment, Submit, the success screen.
6. My Evaluations: the history list.
7. Profile: name, course, year and section.
8. Supervisor sign-in, the SEF faculty list, and rating one faculty member.
9. Airplane mode → Submit anyway → airplane mode off → the "Your evaluation has been sent" notification.
10. A reminder notification, for example "Faculty evaluation is now open".

### Web admin dashboard

1. Dashboard overview, then recording while a phone submits, so the numbers change live.
2. Evaluation Control: Submission Tracking and the deadline.
3. Teachers page: sorted list, the Role column, a dual-role supervisor.
4. Students page: arranged by section; a bulk CSV upload finishing.
5. Reports & Analytics: open a faculty member's Annex C, then Annex D.
6. Generate FER and Export PDF: the printed page with signatures.
7. Department page: the 5-row preview, View all, Export PDF.
8. Feedback: anonymous comments (Respondent 1, 2…), Print Ratings.

### B-roll (non-screen footage)

- Paper forms on a desk; a hand tallying with a calculator.
- Students on campus using their phones.
- The evaluation office at work; a printer printing a report.
- The NwSSU building or signage (ask permission before filming people).

## 5. Production guide

### Look and feel

- **Colours:** use the system's own palette: deep forest green `#064E3B`, emerald `#059669`, white and light grey. The splash screen colour is a natural background for titles.
- **Logo:** the NwSSU seal on a white circle, the same as the app's splash screen.
- **Fonts:** one clean sans-serif for every title, for example Poppins, Inter or Montserrat. Keep text short: 3–6 words per overlay.
- **Transitions:** simple cuts and slides. Zoom into the part of the screen being explained.

### Music

- Calm, uplifting corporate or tech track, about 100–120 BPM, quieter under the voice-over.
- Use royalty-free music only, for example the YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music or the CapCut library, so the video isn't muted or blocked when uploaded.

### Recording

- **Phone:** Android's built-in screen recorder (Quick Settings → Screen record), or mirror to a laptop with scrcpy and record there for a sharper picture.
- **Admin:** OBS Studio or the Xbox Game Bar (Windows + G) at 1920×1080. Zoom the browser to 110–125% so text is readable.
- Record each shot two or three times, slowly, with short pauses before and after every tap; it is easier to cut than to re-record.
- Hide personal things: bookmarks bar, other tabs, notifications, real student data.

### Voice-over

- Record in a quiet, carpeted room, phone or microphone about a hand-width from the mouth.
- Speak slower than feels natural and smile while speaking; it sounds warmer.
- Optional: a Filipino or Waray-Waray version of the same script for students.

### Editing

- Free tools that work well: CapCut (desktop or phone) or DaVinci Resolve.
- Add burned-in captions; CapCut's auto-captions save time, but correct the spelling of names and terms (SET, SEF, CMO, Annex).
- Export the main video at 1080p, 16:9, and the short at 1080×1920, 9:16.

### Before you publish

- [ ] No real names, IDs, ratings or comments visible anywhere.
- [ ] Permission from anyone filmed on campus, and from the university if the logo is used publicly.
- [ ] Adviser and team names spelled correctly in the credits.
- [ ] Captions checked against the voice-over.
- [ ] Watched once on a phone with the sound off: the message is still clear.
- [ ] Music is royalty-free and credited if the licence asks for it.
